Our Mission

We turn excess into access and compassion into measurable wellness outcomes.

TWI is a humanitarian NGO built to reduce preventable suffering by responsibly recovering usable supplies, mobilizing wellness professionals, and equipping under-resourced communities with tools for long-term health. Our work prioritizes partnership, cultural humility, transparency, and local ownership.

Our focus

Four practical pathways to healthier communities

Health Access

Medical consumables, mobility aids, hygiene kits, preventive screenings, and training for community health teams.

Clean Water

Water filtration, sanitation education, and locally maintainable water projects that reduce disease risk.

Wellness Education

Age-appropriate resources on nutrition, maternal health, mental wellbeing, first aid, and preventive care.

Community Resilience

Volunteer networks, emergency readiness, women-centered wellness kits, and livelihood support for families.

How it works

Recover. Prepare. Deliver. Sustain.

  1. 01

    Recover

    We collect surplus, unopened, and gently used wellness supplies from hospitals, clinics, businesses, and households.

  2. 02

    Prepare

    Volunteers inspect, sort, inventory, and package items by community-requested categories and safety standards.

  3. 03

    Deliver

    Regional partners coordinate shipments, last-mile delivery, and accountable handoff documentation.

  4. 04

    Sustain

    We pair deliveries with training, monitoring, and recurring support that strengthens local wellness systems.
